About Fabulaze Studio

Affordable Beauty Treatments in Cape Town

LET’S TALK ABOUT BEAUTY

Fabulaze Beauty Studio started as a Covid baby in 2020 by two best friends Danica & Anika.

Danica, a qualified Play Therapist, and Anika, a Hairdresser, had to find a way to support themselves when Schools & Hairdressers were forced to stay closed. They transformed the Play therapy office into a beauty Studio & only offered Permanent Laser Hair Removal, Laser Lipo Treatments & Eyelash Extensions.

Fabulaze has grown with lots of training, & another lady boss joining the team. We now offer a wide range of treatments from Acrylic Nails, Waxing, Spray Tan to Semi Permanent Make up & many more. We also have a Botox day every month.

The difference between Fabulaze and some other salons is the warm, casual feeling in our open plan Studio where we have chats and laughs — you are treated as family or a friend. We want everybody to feel welcome, also those clients that do not feel comfortable in a high-end salon.

Our motto is "We make Beauty Affordable" and that is why we strive to keep our prices affordable, our products the best quality, our Service fabulaze and the best quality workmanship.

At Fabulaze Beauty Studio you will be made to feel fabulaze. We look forward to meeting you!
