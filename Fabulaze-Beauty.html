About Fabulaze Studio

Affordable Beauty Treatments in Cape Town

LET’S TALK ABOUT BEAUTY

Fabulaze Beauty Studio started as a Covid baby in 2020 by two best friends Danica & Anika.

Danica, a qualified Play Therapist, and Anika, a Hairdresser, had to find a way to support themselves when Schools & Hairdressers were forced to stay closed. They transformed the Play therapy office into a beauty Studio & only offered Permanent Laser Hair Removal, Laser Lipo Treatments & Eyelash Extensions.

Fabulaze has grown with lots of training, & another lady boss joining the team. We now offer a wide range of treatments from Acrylic Nails, Waxing, Spray Tan to Semi Permanent Make up & many more. We also have a Botox day every month.

The difference between Fabulaze and some other salons is the warm, casual feeling in our open plan Studio where we have chats and laughs — you are treated as family or a friend. We want everybody to feel welcome, also those clients that do not feel comfortable in a high-end salon.

Our motto is "We make Beauty Affordable" and that is why we strive to keep our prices affordable, our products the best quality, our Service fabulaze and the best quality workmanship.

At Fabulaze Beauty Studio you will be made to feel fabulaze. We look forward to meeting you!

Elite Custom Cosmetics

Flawless Definition

Our permanent cosmetics and aesthetic solutions combine medical-grade engineering with expert artistic geometry. By embedding premium hypoallergenic pigments and utilizing precise application protocols, we design low-maintenance, long-lasting definitions custom-tailored to enhance your natural facial architecture.

Why Choose Fabulaze PMU?

  • ✔ Symmetrical, bespoke mapping tailored to your unique bone structure
  • ✔ Medical-grade topical anesthetics used for optimal procedural comfort
  • ✔ Premium, color-stable pigments that resist uneven fading over time

Achieve perfectly sculpted, symmetrical arches with our industry-leading eyebrow enhancement treatments in Cape Town. We specialize in both microblading hair-stroke replication for a crisp, natural appearance and ombré powder brow shading for a soft, filled-in makeup aesthetic.

Using advanced color theory and precision face-mapping techniques, we customize pigment depth and undertones to harmonize beautifully with your skin tone and hair color. This service is ideal for correcting sparse areas, defining asymmetric brow structures, or completely restoring over-tweezed arches with long-lasting, smudgeproof results.

Define your eyes and create the illusion of thicker, darker eyelashes with our premium permanent eyeliner cosmetic tattooing. From subtle, soft lash-line enhancements that gently trace the base of your lashes to bold, classic winged eyeliner designs, our procedures eliminate the daily frustration of smudging, running cosmetics.

We utilize ultra-fine, sterile needle modules and ophthalmologically stable pigments to safely deliver sharp lines along the delicate eyelid margin. Perfect for active individuals, contact lens wearers, or anyone seeking effortless ocular definition that remains immaculate through workouts, swimming, and humid weather.

Revitalize lost color margins and infuse your lips with a soft, natural flush of everyday pigment using our targeted lip blush tattooing treatment. This specialized technique redefines asymmetrical lip borders, creates the optical appearance of fuller volume through uniform shade saturation, and provides complete waterproof wearability.

We also offer advanced melanin lip neutralization services to gently balance cool, dark undertones into warm, uniform pink or peach palettes. The result is a beautifully blended, translucent stain effect that enhances natural lip tissue architecture while minimizing the need for constant topical lipstick reapplication.

Smooth away fine expression lines and restore youthful vitality during our highly sought-after monthly Botox and anti-wrinkle injector events. Administered by a certified, medical professional, these targeted cosmetic injectable treatments temporarily relax muscle groups responsible for dynamic wrinkling along the forehead, crow's feet, and frown lines.

By maintaining strict clinical safety standards within our comfortable boutique studio environment, we make advanced medical aesthetics approachable and seamless. Secure your spot in our monthly cycles to enjoy precise dosages, professional structural assessments, and subtle, natural-looking facial rejuvenation.
